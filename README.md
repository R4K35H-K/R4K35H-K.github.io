## About
I am a researcher specializing in **Multimedia Security**, **Steganalysis**, and **Robust JPEG Steganography**. My work focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical information concealment and the realities of modern social networking platforms. 

## Key Highlights
* **Research Focus:** Developing content-adaptive, resilient steganographic algorithms that withstand aggressive platform recompression.
* **Innovations:** Creator of four novel schemes: **SARAS**, **MRAS**, **CEDAR**, and **DRAS**.
* **Publications:** Multiple peer-reviewed articles in top-tier journals (Q1 SCIE/Scopus), including *Signal Processing* and *J. Visual Communication & Image Representation*.
